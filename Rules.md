YourRage:
Requirements
Before you post your first clip, review these requirements. Videos that don't meet every requirement won't be approved for payout.

Content must be sourced from YourRAGE's GTA 6 marathon stream [https://www.twitch.tv/yourragegaming ]
Submitted videos must feature Rage as the primary focus of the video.
Videos can only be posted on TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts.
Audience must be at least 50% native English-speaking countries (USA, CA, AU, UK, NZ).
Your caption/text overlay must mention Rage's name
Your captions must use the hashtag: #yourrage
Accounts usual audience may not be in a vastly different niche. (e.g posting {YourRAGE} clips on a bird watching profile may make your video subject to rejection).
Only Approved videos count, must meet TOTAL VIEW REQUIREMENT to participate in the Revenue share
Campaign will run the duration of YourRAGE's GTA 6 Marathon stream
Learn how pool campaigns work: https://docs.google.com/document/d/1E6aKQtlztz8aHXfP8B0ohk91ZBcok0DsQz5GLCE9mwM/edit?usp=sharing
Videos cannot portray yourRAGE in a negative light.
Views and engagement cannot be manipulated in any way. (Botting, Reposts, Collab Posts, ETC.)
Logos of any kind are not allowed.
Edited content MUST contain yourRAGE. Videos only featuring yourRAGE in the intro and not the actual edit will be rejected.
Videos CAN NOT “hate farm”, any videos creating a negative false narrative are subject to rejection and in some cases a ban.
No Repetitive/Non Content Matching Thumbnails & Japanese Captions/Descriptions.
What to look for:

Funny moments from yourRAGE's social media accounts
Memorable quotes from yourRAGE's social media accounts
Other reactions or memorable moments from yourRAGE's social media accounts

YourRAGE Twitch
Get paid
All payouts run through Whop's Content Rewards. Here's the flow from posting to cash-out.

Open The Content Rewards App
You'll find it inside the Whop Community. This is where you submit your clip links and track approval status.
Verify Your Whop Account
Before you can be paid, you need to verify your account by connecting your socials. Go to Whop → Settings → Connected Accounts.
Submit Your Video Links
Once verified, head back to Content Rewards and paste the link to every yourRAGE clip you've posted. Submit within 30 minutes of posting.
Revenue Share
Approved views add up across all your clips. When the campaign ends, the $5,000 pool is split by each clipper's share of approved views.
Questions? Open a support ticket in the Reach Discord. For Whop-specific issues such as payouts or account verification, contact Whop support.

Account creation
If you don't already have a relevant clipping page with 100+ followers, we strongly recommend setting up a dedicated yourRAGE clipping account.

If you have a pre-existing account in a relevant niche, you can skip account creation.

Create Fresh Accounts
Make new accounts on TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube.
Pick a Campaign-Related Username
Use a name related to yourRAGE. Examples: yourRAGEClips, yourRAGEDaily, yourRAGEMoments, or yourRAGEReels.
Set a Profile Picture
Use the campaign thumbnail or official yourRAGE artwork.
Targeting USA
To help meet the audience requirement:

Use English-language captions and on-screen text.
Avoid country-specific slang from outside the target regions.
Tag US locations only when relevant to the content.
If you have access to audience insights, check whether your historical audience is primarily from the USA, UK, CA, AU, or NZ.
Final rules
Meet every quality standard listed in the Requirements article.
Submit yourRAGE clips within 30 minutes of posting.
Read every section of this page before posting your first clip.
