# Privacy Policy

**App:** TikTok Uploader

**Effective date:** November 18, 2026

## Information handled

TikTok Uploader processes local video files, generated captions, OAuth credentials, API tokens, clip metadata, and posting status. Source and generated media are processed locally on the operator's device unless the operator chooses to upload it through YouTube or TikTok.

## OAuth and account data

YouTube and TikTok authorization may provide account identifiers, access tokens, refresh tokens, and publishing permissions. These credentials are stored locally in files such as `yt_token.json` and `tiktok_token.json`. They are not intentionally transmitted to any third party other than the respective platform API.

The operator must keep credential files private. They must not be added to source control or shared publicly.

## Uploads

When the operator runs the upload function, selected clip files, titles, descriptions, and publishing settings are sent to the selected platform. Platform data handling is governed by YouTube's or TikTok's privacy policy.

## Generated moderation data

The app may analyze local frames for nudity and speech for profanity or safety filtering. Analysis results are not intentionally sent to a separate moderation service.

## Retention and deletion

Local source videos, generated clips, captions, queue records, and tokens remain until the operator deletes them. Source deletion is disabled by default and requires explicit configuration or a forced cleanup command.
